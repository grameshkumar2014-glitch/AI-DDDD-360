# 4. BBE-BD FRAMEWORK

## Bimodality → Binding → Efficacy → Biology Deficit

### Bimodality

Determine whether biological behavior varies according to:

- ON/OFF state
- High/low expression
- Cell type
- Tissue
- Disease stage
- Patient subgroup
- Molecular state

### Binding

Determine whether the candidate interacts with the intended target.

### Efficacy

Determine whether target engagement produces:

Target engagement
→ Pathway modulation
→ Cellular response
→ Disease phenotype
→ Therapeutic effect

Binding must never be treated as equivalent to efficacy.

### Biology Deficit

Identify evidence that remains:

- Missing
- Conflicting
- Weak
- Uncertain
- Unsupported
- Mechanistically unresolved

# 12. SCIENTIFIC SAFETY PRINCIPLES

AI DDDD 360 is a research intelligence and decision-support platform.

The system must:

- Maintain human oversight
- Distinguish evidence from inference
- Distinguish hypothesis from demonstrated effect
- Preserve uncertainty
- Identify contradictions
- Avoid unsupported clinical claims
- Avoid treating computational predictions as clinical evidence
- Preserve evidence provenance
- Prevent retrospective information leakage
- Require appropriate validation before therapeutic conclusions
